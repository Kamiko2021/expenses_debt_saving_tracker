# Privacy Policy for Smart Financial Manager Application

**Last Updated: May 23, 2024**

Smart Financial Manager Application ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and share information when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Data
When you create an account, we may collect:
*   **Email Address:** Used for account authentication and communication.
*   **Authentication Data:** Provided through Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication.

### 1.2 Financial Data
The app allows you to manually input financial information, including:
*   Expenses, Income, Debts, and Savings Goals.
*   Transaction dates and categories.

### 1.3 Device Information
We may collect information about your mobile device, such as:
*   Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.
*   Push notification tokens (via Firebase Cloud Messaging) to send you alerts.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:
*   **To provide the service:** Managing your account and syncing your financial data across devices.
*   **AI Analysis:** We use the Google Gemini AI API to provide financial insights and categorizations based on the data you provide.
*   **Notifications:** To send you reminders or updates regarding your financial tracking.
*   **Security:** To detect and prevent fraud or unauthorized access.

## 3. Data Storage and Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services which may process your data:

*   **Firebase (Google):** We use Firebase Authentication for logins, Cloud Firestore for database storage, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications. Data is stored on Google's secure servers.
*   **Google Gemini AI:** Financial data may be processed by Google's AI models to generate insights.
*   **Google Play Billing:** For processing any in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## 4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We only share information:
*   With service providers (like Google Firebase) necessary to run the app.
*   If required by law or to protect our legal rights.

## 5. Your Rights and Choices

*   **Access and Update:** You can view and edit your financial data directly within the app.
*   **Account Deletion:** You may request account deletion by contacting us or using the in-app options (if available), which will remove your data from Firebase.
*   **Push Notifications:** You can opt-out of notifications through your device settings.

## 6. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures provided by Firebase to protect your data. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
